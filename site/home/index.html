    How it works

    Why the browser needs a bridge at home

    A web page cannot discover or talk to devices on your WiFi: browsers have no raw network sockets, block public sites from calling 192.168.x.x addresses, and this site allows no outside requests at all. The VM is in Azure, not in your house. So a small bridge runs at home and connects out to the VM; nothing on the router is opened.

    Setup
    1. On the VM, once: sudo sh ~/portfolio/server/home-relay-install.sh. It prints the page password and the bridge token.
    2. On a machine at home that stays on (laptop, Raspberry Pi, or an Android phone with Termux): 
      curl -O http://68.221.25.41/home/bridge.py
python3 bridge.py setup
python3 bridge.py scan
python3 bridge.py
    3. Optional cloud appliances: a SmartThings token from account.smartthings.com/tokens, or a Home Assistant long-lived token. setup asks for both.
    4. Devices the scan misses go in bridge.json: 
      "devices": [
  {"driver": "samsung", "host": "192.168.1.40", "name": "Hall TV"},
  {"driver": "wol", "mac": "AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF", "name": "Desktop"}
]
    5. Keep it running: nohup python3 bridge.py &, a systemd service, or Termux:Boot on Android. Give the TV a fixed IP in the router.
    commands expire after 15 s, so nothing turns on late · the bridge only talks to devices it found or you listed
